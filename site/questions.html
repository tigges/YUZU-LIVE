First-time client & consultation quick guide

1. Walk-in 48h patch test (colour & balayage)

Pop into reception any Tuesday–Saturday at least 48 hours before any colour or balayage service. It takes 2 minutes and needs no appointment.

2. Choose your stylist tier & book

Every appointment begins with a bespoke consultation. Select Creative Director, Senior Stylist, Stylist, or Junior Stylist on our price list, then book on Phorest or call 020 8840 2244.

How much is a haircut at YUZU hair in Ealing?

A ladies wash, cut and style is £45 with a junior stylist, £60 with a stylist, £75 with a senior stylist and £90 with a creative director. Kids start at £15. Blow-dries start at £35.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes. As part of our updated colour line, we now require a mandatory patch test for all clients before any colour service, including both existing and new clients. Please complete it at least 48 hours before the appointment. You can walk in even if you do not have a booking.

Where is the salon?

5 Dickens Yard, Longfield Avenue, Ealing, W5 2TD — a short walk from Ealing Broadway station. This is YUZU hair in Ealing, not YUZUHAIR in Hucknall.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Friday, 10am to 8pm. Saturday, 9am to 6pm. Monday and Sunday closed.

How do I book?

Book on Phorest or call 020 8840 2244. Choose creative director, senior stylist, stylist, or junior stylist so the price matches the chair. Colour guests should complete a patch test at least 48 hours first.

What is your cancellation and deposit policy?

We ask for 48 working hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule so your deposit remains refundable or transferable. Because we are closed on Sundays and Mondays, changes to Tuesday appointments must be made by the end of Thursday.

What offers and loyalty rewards do you run?

Colour Tuesdays and Colour Thursdays give 50% off your most expensive colour service with a full priced wash, cut and blow-dry (with a Creative Director on Tuesdays, a Senior Stylist and Stylist on Thursdays). Smooth Wednesdays give 25% off our three to six month smoothing treatment. We also offer £10 Refer a Friend credit, 10% off when you rebook on the same day, and a 4 blow-drys for £156 package.
