Balayage and highlights in Ealing

Freehand balayage, foiliage and classic highlights, finished with a bespoke toner and soft root blending.

Balayage is painted by hand for a soft, sun-lit grow-out. Foiliage and classic foil highlights give brighter, more even lift. We often mix the two so the result looks natural from every side.

Toner and root blending are where the finish is made, so we plan them from the start. As with all colour, please have a patch test at least 48 hours before.

Questions

What is the difference between balayage and highlights?

Balayage is painted freehand for a soft, natural grow-out. Highlights are woven into foils for a brighter, more even result. Foiliage combines the two.

Is toner included?

Toner and blending are part of our highlight and balayage services. Anything extra your hair may need is listed under “Added when the hair needs it” in the prices above.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, at least 48 hours before, for new and existing clients. You can walk in for it.

Which stylist should I book?

Creative Directors and Senior Stylists are the usual choice for a first balayage or a big change. Tap a level above to see the price for each chair.
