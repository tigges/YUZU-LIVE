Haircuts in Ealing

Ladies, gents and children’s cuts in a calm, Japanese-inspired salon at Dickens Yard, a short walk from Ealing Broadway station.

Every cut starts with a consultation: how you wear your hair, how much time you give it in the morning, and what has not worked before. Then we wash, cut and finish it so you can see how it sits.

Pick the chair that suits you. Creative Directors and Senior Stylists take on restyles and precision shapes; Stylists and Junior Stylists are a lighter-priced way to keep a shape you love.

Questions

How much is a haircut at YUZU hair?

It depends on the stylist level and who the cut is for. The price list above shows ladies, gents and children’s cuts for every level; tap a level to see just that column.

Do you cut men’s and children’s hair?

Yes. We cut gents’ hair, including fades, and children and teens of all ages.

Can I book a blow-dry on its own?

Yes. Blow-dries can be booked on their own, and our 4 blow-drys package saves on regular appointments.

How do I choose a stylist level?

Creative Directors suit big changes and precise shapes; Senior Stylists bring advanced cutting experience; Stylists are fully qualified for all cuts; Junior Stylists work under senior guidance at a lower price.
