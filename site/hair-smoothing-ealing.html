Hair smoothing in Ealing

Lakmé Aura smoothing that lasts three to six months, 100% formaldehyde and ammonia free, plus Nashi and K2.0 moisture treatments.

Looking for a keratin treatment? Aura smoothing calms frizz and cuts drying time while keeping movement in the hair, without formaldehyde. It is priced by length, from short to long, with an upgrade that lasts longer.

For hair that needs strength and shine rather than smoothing, Nashi filler therapy and K2.0 intense moisture are quicker treatments that can be added to any visit.

Questions

How long does Aura smoothing last?

Three to six months, depending on the option you choose and how you care for your hair at home.

Is it formaldehyde free?

Yes. Lakmé Aura smoothing is 100% formaldehyde and ammonia free.

What is Smooth Wednesdays?

Every Wednesday you get 25% off our three to six month smoothing treatment.

How is the price worked out?

By the length of your hair, from short to long. The price list above shows each length and the longer-lasting upgrade.
