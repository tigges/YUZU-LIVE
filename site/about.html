Where tradition meets trend, at Dickens Yard.

Welcome

Inspired by the elegance of Japanese culture, Yuzu Hair & Beauty works at 5 Dickens Yard, Longfield Avenue, Ealing, W5 2TD — a short walk from Ealing Broadway station. From blow-outs to sleek cuts, our skilled stylists craft each style with precision and passion.

Open Tuesday to Friday 10am to 8pm and Saturday 9am to 6pm. Monday and Sunday closed. Guests describe the room as calm, and one Google review calls Jasmine “a brilliant artist working in hair”.
