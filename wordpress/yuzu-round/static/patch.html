Mandatory patch testing

As part of our updated colour line, we now require a mandatory patch test for all clients before any colour service, including both existing and new clients.

At Yuzu Hair, we prioritise the safety and well-being of our clients. As part of our updated colour line, we now require a mandatory patch test (allergy alert test) for all clients before any colour service, including both existing and new clients.

The patch test checks for potential allergic reactions to hair dye by applying a small amount to a discreet area of skin (behind the ear) and monitoring for irritation or redness over 48 hours.

Please ensure the patch test is completed at least 48 hours prior to your appointment. We encourage you to walk in for a patch test even if you do not have a scheduled appointment. Simply request one at reception.

Thank you, the Yuzu team.
