Hair colour in Ealing

Roots, tints and full head colour, including Wella Illumina, from colourists who take time over tone and shine.

Every colour guest, new or returning, needs a patch test at least 48 hours before the appointment. You can walk in for one at any time we are open.

Questions

Do I need a patch test before colour?

Yes. All clients, existing and new, need a patch test at least 48 hours before any colour service. Walk in for one, no appointment needed.

What are Colour Tuesdays and Colour Thursdays?

On Tuesdays with a Creative Director, and on Thursdays with a Senior Stylist and Stylist, you get 50% off your most expensive colour service when you book it with a full priced wash, cut & blow-dry.

Which colour brand do you use?

Our colour line includes Wella Illumina, chosen for shine and natural-looking tone.

Can you cover grey hair?

Yes. Root touch-ups and full head tints are both used to cover grey; your colourist will suggest which suits your hair.
