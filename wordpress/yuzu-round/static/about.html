Welcome to Dickens Yard

Inspired by the elegance of Japanese culture, YUZU hair works at 5 Dickens Yard, Longfield Avenue, Ealing, W5 2TD — a short walk from Ealing Broadway station. From blow-outs to sleek cuts, our skilled stylists craft each style with precision and passion.

Located in Dickens Yard behind Ealing Town Hall, a 4-minute walk from Ealing Broadway station (Elizabeth, Central and District lines) with Dickens Yard underground car park right below the courtyard. Open Tuesday to Friday 10am to 8pm, Saturday 9am to 6pm; closed Monday and Sunday. Guests describe the room as calm, and one Google review calls Jasmine “a brilliant artist working in hair”.
