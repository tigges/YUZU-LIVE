Questions

How much is a haircut at Yuzu in Ealing?

A ladies wash, cut and style is £58 with a stylist and £87 with a senior stylist. A gents wash, cut and style is £58. Kids aged 12 and under start at £29. Blow-dries start at £41.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes. As part of our updated colour line, we now require a mandatory patch test for all clients before any colour service, including both existing and new clients. Please complete it at least 48 hours before the appointment. You can walk in even if you do not have a booking.

Where is the salon?

5 Dickens Yard, Longfield Avenue, Ealing, W5 2TD — a short walk from Ealing Broadway station. This is Yuzu Hair & Beauty in Ealing, not YUZUHAIR in Hucknall.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Friday, 10am to 8pm. Saturday, 9am to 6pm. Monday and Sunday closed.

How do I book?

Book on Phorest or call 020 8840 2244. Say whether you want a senior stylist or stylist so the price matches the chair. Colour guests should book the patch test first.

How do I join the team?

Think you’ve got what it takes? We welcome senior stylists, stylists, and models. Email your CV to info@yuzuhairandbeauty.co.uk or see the join our team page.
