Privacy

How this website and the salon handle your details.

Who we are

Yuzu Hair & Beauty, 5 Dickens Yard, Longfield Avenue, Ealing, W5 2TD. Email info@yuzuhairandbeauty.co.uk. Phone 020 8840 2244.

This website

These pages tell you about the salon, our services and price list, current offers, and how to visit. They do not take card payments, and they do not ask you to create an account. They do not run advertising or tracking cookies.

Booking

Book opens Phorest, the salon’s booking service. Phorest handles the appointment, any deposit, and the SMS or email reminders. Their own privacy notice applies once you are on that site. We use booking details to provide the appointment and to meet the cancellation terms.

Messages you send us

If you call, email, or use the Message us contact form or the Careers (Send us your CV) form, we use what you send only to reply to you, arrange your visit, or consider your application. A CV attached to the Careers form is sent with your message to the salon and kept only for recruitment. We do not sell your messages or contact details.

Maps, reviews and social networks

Get directions, Leave a review, and All Google reviews open Google Maps and Google Reviews in a new tab. Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook links also leave this site. Those external services set their own cookies and have their own privacy notices once you open them. Our in-salon review QR and NFC links (/review) redirect to Google Reviews and record only anonymous scan totals by channel and day, without storing cookies, IP addresses, or personal data.

Ask us

Questions about your details can go to info@yuzuhairandbeauty.co.uk or 020 8840 2244.
